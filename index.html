03 · The limits

What remains unknown?

Observed breakups and radar surveys leave gaps when we ask what a particular destructive event would do.

01

Breakup inputs

Fragment count, mass, and ejection speed depend on the target and how it is destroyed. Well-characterized destructive events cover only some of those combinations.

02

Small fragment tracking

Pieces too small to track one by one are estimated from radar samples and size models. Their number and properties remain uncertain.
